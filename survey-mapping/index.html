SURVEYING AND CARTOGRAPHY

Where I’ve Mapped

Southwest Colorado Mapping Project (2023)

This project involved field-based total station mapping of Ancestral Pueblo sites across Montezuma County, Colorado. The focus was on establishing control points, recording architectural features, and integrating results with a broader regional settlement model. A total of 38 sites were documented in collaboration with CRM partners.

San Pedro River Survey (2022)

Conducted intensive pedestrian survey along the upper San Pedro River corridor. Survey teams recorded ephemeral artifact scatters, irrigation features, and historic trails. Results were published in a project report submitted to the BLM.
