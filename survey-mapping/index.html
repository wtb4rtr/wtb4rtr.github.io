MAPPING PROJECTS

Classic Maya Residences | Alacranes Bajo/Blue Creek, Northwestern Belize (2008-2014)

Basemaps:
🟩 Close Up: Stamen Terrain
⚪ Overview: Esri Gray Canvas

In addition to serving as an archaeological crew chief on the excavation of several Classic Maya elite residences in northwestern Belize, I was also responsible for overseeing the total station mapping of approximately 20 sites. Using a Topcon GTS-2R EDM theodolite, I mapped architecture, excavation units, key landscape features, and generated topographic maps. I trained both professional archaeologists and students in total station operation and field drafting techniques for plan and profile recording. I also managed all spatial data using Esri ArcGIS and ArcMap, ensuring accurate integration with the broader site maps and research goals. I assisted Bob Warden of Texas A&M University in LiDAR-based mapping to support topographic and architectural analysis.
