MAPPING PROJECTS

Classic Maya Residences | Alacranes Bajo/Blue Creek, Northwestern Belize (2008-2014)

Basemaps:
🟩 Close Up: Stamen Terrain
⚪ Overview: Esri Gray Canvas

Nojol Nah is a large Classic Maya community composed of multiple elite residential courtyards in the Blue Creek region of northwestern Belize; I worked at eight loci from 2008 - 2014. In addition to serving as an archaeological crew chief on the excavation of these residences, I was also responsible for overseeing the total station mapping of 24 courtyards in Nojol Nah and adjacent communties. Using a Topcon GTS-2R EDM theodolite, I mapped architecture, excavation units, key landscape features, and generated topographic maps. I trained both professional archaeologists and students in total station operation and field drafting techniques for plan and profile recording. I also managed all spatial data using Esri ArcGIS and ArcMap, ensuring accurate integration with the broader site maps and research goals. In addition to the mapping, I assisted Bob Warden of Texas A&M University in 3D laser scanning and LiDAR scanning of several sites.
